How to customize shortcut keys in Firefox

Firefox manages extension shortcuts natively. To remap Alt+S to your preferred keys:

  1. Type about:addons in your Firefox address bar and press Enter
  2. Click the gear icon (⚙) at the top right of the page
  3. Select "Manage Extension Shortcuts"
  4. Find Sticky and enter your custom shortcut
